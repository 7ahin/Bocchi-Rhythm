# MVP Requirements

FR-001: The game shall provide four rhythm lanes.

FR-002: The player shall use A, S, D and F for lanes 1-4.

FR-003: Notes shall move toward a judgement line.

FR-004: Notes shall be spawned based on chart timing data.

FR-005: The game shall play music during gameplay.

FR-006: Player input shall be compared against note timestamps.

FR-007: Judgements shall include Perfect, Great, Good and Miss.

FR-008: Successful hits shall increase combo.

FR-009: Misses shall reset combo.

FR-010: The game shall calculate a score.

FR-011: The game shall show a result screen after a song ends.

FR-012: The player shall be able to pause and restart gameplay.
